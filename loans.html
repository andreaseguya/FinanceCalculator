How this estimate works

Assumes a fixed annual interest rate and equal monthly payments over the selected term.

For the calculation, the annual rate is divided by 12 and the term in years is multiplied by 12. Monthly payment = principal × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate)−number of payments).

At 0% interest, the loan amount is divided equally across the payments. Fees, taxes, and variable rates are not included. Compare the estimate with the lender’s full offer before making a decision.
